# Individual assignments in the middle of columns seven and eight

Let `a=C(n,7)=C(m,8)≥10^136`, and assign each prime divisor of `a` in each column to any numerator term where its valuation is greatest. One prime selects both `n−3` and `m−3`. A different prime selects both `n−3` and `m−4`. The two positions belong to one of the 28 previously established intersections, so every such common value has at least **29 distinct prime divisors**. The conclusion also holds at every possible height in each remaining four-column pattern by their finite height exclusions. [The 28 grouped intersections](Erdos849SevenEightCellsSieve.md) · [Four-column height bounds](Erdos849PatternHeights.md)

For `j=3,4`, put `y=m−j`. If the pair `(3,j)` is empty, the raw prime valuation bound makes `Z=117600a/((n−3)y)` integral. A branch certificate supplies 55 coefficients of an integer-valued polynomial `G(Z)=Σ c_t binom(Z,t)`. Reduce `G` using `8(n)_7=(m)_8` and `7! yZ=117600∏_{s≠3}(n−s)`. Its regular part `P(n,y)` takes integer values by a finite Newton difference test. The remainder `F=G−P` is therefore an integer. Its nonzero terms `n^u y^v` have `0≤u<7`, `v<0` and strictly negative weight `8u+7v`. Bounding the slope `n/y^(8/7)` above and below gives a uniform upper bound for `|F|` and, when the largest term dominates, determines its sign. [Raw auxiliary integer and archimedean bound](Erdos849FiveEightRawBranches.md)

The verifier enumerates all 866 admissible residue pairs modulo 761 and checks each branch independently. The residue `(n,m)=(3,j)` is impossible under the missing-pair assumption by the second expression for `Z`, namely `8!(n−3)Z=117600∏_{s≠j}(m−s)`. Each remaining residue pair has a nonvanishing partial derivative. Choose a free coordinate of the form `x₀+761T` and lift the dependent coordinate. As a series in `761T`, both the dependent coordinate and `F` have coefficients in the 761-adic integers. Here one of the two denominators `n−3,y` is invertible. Modulo `761^E` the resulting function is a polynomial in `T` of degree less than `E`. Testing divisibility on `T=0,…,E−1` proves it for every integer `T` by finite differences. Thus `F/761^E` is an integer on that whole branch.

For each branch the verifier sieves the finite interval for this integer against the possible values of `F/761^E` at other primes. The residue sets are exact images of the defining curve and auxiliary expression. The verifier cross-checks the packed computation by direct evaluation at two test primes for each orientation. The reflection `m↦7−m` takes `m−j` and `Z` to their negatives; the verifier transforms the binomial coefficients and repeats the branch checks at the other offset. No integer survives.

The largest absolute integer bound among the 866 branches is 543,075 for both orientations. Run `python -B Erdos849SevenEightRawBranches.py` with the adjacent compressed certificate. All verification uses the Python standard library.
